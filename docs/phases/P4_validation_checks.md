# P4 — Validation and checks

**Goal:** a generated `docs/CHECKS.md` that shows the estimates behave like Delhi's buses, plus independent ground truth from the October GPS feed. **Time box:** 3 h (P4b 1.5 h + P4 1.5 h).
**Lane:** C (background `line-cook`); `sous-chef` reviews the GPS logic. **Depends on:** P1 for the profiles; P2 and P3 for everything segment-level; the GPS archive (P0) for P4b.

Every number and chart is generated by `python -m seatcast.run checks` from `data/out/` and `data/work/`. Nothing is typed by hand (CLAUDE.md §2 rule 2).

## Charts (dataviz rules)
- One y-axis per chart, never dual. Thin marks; 2 px lines. Recessive grid and axes. A legend for ≥ 2 series, plus selective direct labels.
- Series colours in fixed order from the validated categorical palette: weekday `#2a78d6`, Saturday `#eb6834`, Sunday `#1baf7a`. Crowding levels always use the THINKING D-08 scale.
- Text stays in ink colours, never the series colour.
- Matplotlib, system sans, `dpi=200`, saved to `docs/img/*.png`, each with alt text in the markdown.
- Look at every rendered PNG once for collisions before calling it done.

## P4b — Ground truth from GPS (run first; it can start as soon as P1 is done)

1. **Extract three days** (non-solid archive, so only these are read). Wed 14 Oct, Sat 17 Oct and Sun 11 Oct sit outside the festival week.
   ```powershell
   & "C:\Program Files\7-Zip\7z.exe" x data\raw\gps\delhi-dtc-bus-gtfs-rt-2026-10.7z -odata\work\gps bus_movements_2026_10_14.db bus_movements_2026_10_17.db bus_movements_2026_10_11.db
   ```
2. **To parquet, once** (DuckDB's sqlite scanner, no indexes needed):
   ```sql
   INSTALL sqlite; LOAD sqlite;
   COPY (SELECT DISTINCT vehicle_id v, CAST(lat AS DOUBLE) lat, CAST(lng AS DOUBLE) lon,
                CAST(route_id AS BIGINT) route_id, trip_id, CAST(timestamp AS BIGINT) t_utc
         FROM sqlite_scan('data/work/gps/bus_movements_2026_10_14.db', 'vehicle_feed'))
   TO 'data/work/gps_2026-10-14.parquet';
   ```
3. **Check the GPS `route_id`.** Does it equal GTFS `route_id`? Pings of a route should lie within ~200 m of that route's stops most of the time. If not, stop P4b steps 4–5 and report it.
4. **Observed vs scheduled service** (THINKING D-05).
   - Observed trips per route and hour = distinct (`v`, `trip_id`) by the hour of their first ping (IST).
   - Scheduled trips = GTFS trips of that route leaving the first stop in that hour.
   - Delivery ratio = observed / scheduled, by route-hour and day type. Show its distribution and the 10 worst high-demand routes.
   - Write `service_scale` candidates to `data/work/service_scale.csv`. Applying them is a P8 decision unless the ratio is far from 1.
5. **Who is in the ticket file** (THINKING D-03). For the same dates:
   - Share of ticket vehicles, and of their passengers, that appear in the DTC-only GPS feed.
   - If about half, the file covers DTC + cluster (keep E central). If nearly all, revert the denominator to DTC.
6. **Where was the bus when the ticket was bought?** This tests "boarding time ≈ `created_at`" and the stop matching together.
   ```sql
   SELECT t.tid, haversine_m(g.lat, g.lon, s.lat, s.lon) d_board, t.t_utc - g.t_utc lag_s
   FROM tix t ASOF JOIN gps g ON t.v = g.v AND t.t_utc >= g.t_utc
   JOIN board_stops s USING (tid)
   WHERE t.t_utc - g.t_utc <= 60;
   ```
   - Report the median and p75/p90 distance, and the share within 300 m and 500 m.
   - As a contrast, report the same for the alighting stop (should be far).
   - Write `haversine_m` as a small SQL macro.
   - Outputs: `data/out/gps_checks.json` and CHECKS §11.

## P4 — `docs/CHECKS.md` sections (CLAUDE.md §10 plus 11–12)

1. **Coverage:** rows, passengers, days, % discounted slabs, % NCR, % NULL vehicle, route/stop/order match rates, agency split.
2. **Expansion:** D_daily, E low/central/high, and one sentence on what they mean.
3. **Weekday hourly profile** (chart): peak hours, peak-to-midday ratio. Expect 09:00 and 18:00 with a ratio of about 5.
4. **Weekday vs Saturday vs Sunday** (chart): daily totals by date with holidays marked (2 Oct, 20 Oct).
5. **Busiest corridors:** top 15 segments by daily flow, with stop names.
6. **Directional asymmetry:** UP vs DOWN share in AM vs PM for the top 20 routes. Expect the peak direction to flip.
7. **Week-to-week stability:** distribution of ρ per route (chart); list the unstable routes.
8. **Anomalous days:** table (expect none beyond the reassigned holidays; note 19 Oct at −19%).
9. **Plausibility:** LF distribution across cells (central E); share of cells over seats and over total capacity; top 10 most crowded route-hours; every cell with L > 3 × total flagged.
10. **Where estimates are weakest:** low-n cells, routes with poor match rates, night hours, schedule gaps.
11. **GPS ground truth** (P4b): delivery ratio; DTC vehicle overlap; boarding-position distances.
12. **Sensitivity:** how many cells change level under E low vs high; run_cv 0.25 vs 0.45; an AC/non-AC stratified E using fleet shares (assumption: ridership ∝ fleet). This bounds how much the single-E simplification matters (THINKING D-06).

## Done when

- Sections 1–10 are present. Section 11 is present, or says "not run" with the reason. Section 12 is present.

## If it goes wrong
- **GPS `route_id` doesn't map to GTFS:** keep only §11's vehicle-overlap check (needs no route mapping), say so, and move on.
- **No time:** skip §11's boarding-position check first (cut list item 5), then §12.
